# One Woman Army: from business problem to shipped solution 🥷

Hi! 👋 I'm Agnieszka, an Engineering Manager with a developer's soul.
I've been in software since 2011 and write at [Woman on Rails](https://womanonrails.com/).

I work across the whole path: I talk to the client, find out what they
really need, shape the spec and scope, propose a solution (with AI and
automation where it actually pays off), and lead it all the way to delivery.

**AI writes most of the code these days. I make sure it's the right code:**
solid architecture, a healthy delivery flow, and business assumptions
that hold up.

## What I bring 🧭

- 🗣️ **Client discovery**: turning fuzzy business goals into clear,
  testable requirements
- 🔍 **Judgment**: spotting early what won't work, including AI-generated
  plans that look great on paper
- 🤖 **AI-integrated SDLC**: AI in everyday delivery, from spec to review
- 👥 **Engineering management**: Definition of Done, PR metrics, delivery
  processes that don't need firefighting
- 🏗️ **Technical depth**: Ruby on Rails, TypeScript, Python, architecture, TDD

## What I've done lately 📁

- 🩺 **Platform for clinicians to manage their patients**: set up the delivery
  flow and kept refining it with the team while we experimented with different
  ways of working with AI. Reviewed PRs, took over part of the PO role in client
  communication, coded when needed, and shipped v1.0 to production.
  Now: support & maintenance.
- 🔎 **AI-powered search for industrial machinery**: organized the work, improved
  communication and collaboration with the client, and coded where needed
  (core features, landing page, securing and optimizing AI queries).
  Now: advising the client on next steps such as cost optimization and security.
- 🏋️ **Gym management platform**: led the team and backlog. Now: reviewing
  AI-written PRs, handling client communication, and coordinating the remaining work.
- 🧩 **Internal**: building internal AI skills, testing external AI workflows
  and methods (e.g. [BMAD Method](https://github.com/bmad-code-org/BMAD-METHOD),
  [ECC](https://github.com/affaan-m/everything-claude-code)), sharing knowledge,
  and getting people curious about new skills.
